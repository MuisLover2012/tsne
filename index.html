How to play
Move
Keep one finger on the screen and drag to move.
Swing
Tap with another finger as the ball arrives. The racket swings when you lift it.
Power
A quick tap hits hard and deep. Hold a little longer for a softer shot, or hold until the bar flashes for a drop shot.
Aim
Tap the spot on your opponent's side where you want the ball to land. Tap your own side and the game picks the open court.
Timing
Perfect timing lands on your target. Early or late swings drift off it.
Serve
Tap to toss, then tap again at the top. Two faults lose the point.
