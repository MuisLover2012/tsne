# Grass Court Rally

A tennis game for your phone. Play one short set (first to 4 games, win by two) against a computer opponent on a grass court.

Open `index.html` in a phone browser. It's a single file with no build step and no dependencies.

## How to play

- **Move:** drag anywhere on the screen. Your player follows your finger.
- **Hit:** your racket swings by itself when the ball reaches you.
- **Aim:** flick left or right as you hit. Flick up for a deep, hard shot, or flick hard down for a drop shot.
- **Serve:** tap the screen.

On a computer, use the arrow keys to move, Space to serve and P to pause.

There are three opponents: Easy, Club and Pro. Easy and Club show a marker where the opponent's shot will land.
